What is being tested?

Digit Differs

Select the least frequent last digit using only the previous 100 ticks; score whether the next tick differs. Ties are skipped.

Even / Odd

Select the less frequent parity in the previous window; score the next digit. This tests frequency imbalance, not a guaranteed prediction.

Over / Under

Choose the less frequent half (0–4 or 5–9) from the previous window and score the next tick.

Rise / Fall momentum

Use the direction of the last five price changes to predict the next tick direction. Flat moves are excluded.

Profitability gate — not just win rate

Historical hit rate alone is not enough. Before any strategy can qualify for demo execution, we must obtain the current Deriv proposal for the exact contract, stake, barrier and duration, calculate its break-even probability, then confirm positive expected value on untouched out-of-sample data.

Required checks

Live proposal payout · exact contract rules · confidence interval · enough independent trades · multiple-testing correction · drawdown · demo forward test.

Safety limits reserved for later demo phase

US$3.50 stake ceiling · maximum four completed trades per market · no Martingale · demo USD account only · manual stop control.
